# EcoTrace OSS

EcoTrace OSS is an open-source transparency infrastructure project designed for ethical and sustainable fashion brands.

The goal of the project is to help brands document sourcing, manufacturing stages, materials, certifications, and environmental impact in a transparent and developer-friendly way.

---

## Vision

Modern consumers deserve transparency about:

- Fabric origins
- Manufacturing processes
- Material composition
- Environmental impact
- Supply-chain ethics

EcoTrace OSS aims to provide open tooling that makes transparency accessible to both small and large brands.

---

## Planned Features

- Supply-chain tracking
- Material transparency system
- Environmental impact logging
- Public transparency dashboards
- QR-based product traceability
- Sustainability reporting APIs
- Open documentation standards
